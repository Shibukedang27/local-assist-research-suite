# Safety and privacy boundaries

## Jobs

- Generates local drafts and match explanations only.
- Does not click or invoke a final application submission action.
- Every external submission must be reviewed and performed by the user.
- Never commit resumes, contact details, credentials, browser profiles, or cookies.

## Aptitude and reasoning

- Intended for practice, tutoring, explanations, and accessibility assistance.
- Refuses workflows identified as live, graded, proctored, or recruitment assessments.
- Does not impersonate the user or bypass monitoring.

## Market research

- Produces transparent, fallible research signals with uncertainty.
- Does not connect to brokers, place orders, or promise returns.
- Backtests can overstate future performance; costs and slippage must be considered.

## Public repository rule

Only synthetic examples belong in this repository. Local private data must stay under ignored paths such as `data/private/` or outside the checkout.
